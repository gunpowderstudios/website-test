The cursed forest of Calen Taur awaits.

The familiar Bag of Dungeon idea moves out of the stone corridors and into a dark fantasy wilderness.

Explore as you play, discover what lies ahead and deal with whatever the forest throws at your party. Bag of Dungeon 2 keeps the fast, accessible adventure of the original while giving you a completely different place to get lost in.

Start here if you want a standalone adventure, or bring the original game to the table and connect the two worlds together.
