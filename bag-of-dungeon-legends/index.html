Expand your party

The dungeon gets bigger when the heroes do.

Legends is about adding more variety, more character and more reasons to return to Bag of Dungeon.

Bring additional adventurers into your games and mix up the way your party tackles the dungeon. Then push things further with Legends 2: Relics, adding another layer of fantasy treasure and new possibilities to your adventures.

Rather than treating the two packs as separate worlds, this page keeps the whole Legends range together so players can see how it grows alongside Bag of Dungeon.
