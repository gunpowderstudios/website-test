Your quest

Get in. Get the Ring. Get out alive.

The dungeon is built as you explore, so you never know what is waiting around the next corner.

Choose your adventurer and descend into a randomly generated maze of rooms and corridors. Find weapons, armour and magical items, fight monsters and survive the traps. Somewhere in the darkness lies the Ring of Creation.

Find it, reach the exit and defeat the Red Dragon. Simple objective. Questionable survival rate.

How does it play?

Old-school dungeon crawling without an evening spent reading a rulebook. Lay tiles, make decisions, roll dice and discover the adventure as you go.

1

Choose a hero

Pick your adventurer and enter the dungeon. Each hero gives you a different way to face what is coming.

2

Build as you explore

Draw and place dungeon tiles as you move. The maze grows differently every time you play.

3

Fight & loot

Battle monsters, dodge traps and collect weapons, armour, magic and treasure to stay alive.

4

Escape the Dragon

Find the Ring of Creation, reach the exit and survive the Red Dragon standing between you and freedom.
