Draw. Roll. Survive.

Your dungeon appears beneath your pencil.

No board to set up. No sprawling campaign to organise. Open the book and your next dungeon is waiting.

Book of Dungeon takes the dungeon-crawling feeling of exploration, danger and treasure hunting and puts it straight onto the page. As you move, you draw your route and create a record of the adventure as it unfolds.

Each dungeon gives you a new challenge. Monsters get in the way, traps punish bad luck, treasure keeps you moving and your hero develops as you push deeper into the book.

How to play.

Start with a blank dungeon map and finish with the story of where your hero went — assuming they make it out.

1

Open a dungeon.

Choose your next level, ready your hero and find the entrance.

2

Draw as you explore.

Sketch the dungeon onto the page as rooms, routes and discoveries are revealed.

3

Fight & loot.

Battle monsters, survive hazards and grab whatever treasure you can carry.

4

Keep going.

Take your hero into the next dungeon and see just how many levels they survive.

“How long can your hero survive?”

Every finished page becomes its own hand-drawn record of the adventure — arrows, monsters, treasure, bad decisions and all.

Made for solo adventurers.

Play at the table, on the sofa or take it away with you. Book of Dungeon gives you a complete dungeon-crawling session without needing a large box of components.
