Who Stole My Cheese? is a light family game built around bluffing, suspicion and reading the people around the table. Players have to decide who looks innocent, who looks suspicious and who is simply very good at pretending.

It is designed to create the sort of accusations, laughter and last-second changes of mind that make a social game memorable.

How to play.

Look innocent, spot the tells and work out who has been helping themselves to the cheese.

1

Set the scene.

Everyone gets involved, but not everyone has the same reason to look innocent.

2

Watch the table.

Listen to what people say, notice what they do and decide whose story does not quite add up.

3

Bluff if you must.

Sometimes the best defence is confidence — especially when you have something to hide.

4

Name the culprit.

Make your accusation and find out whether you caught the cheese thief or blamed the wrong mouse.

“Who has been chomping the cheddar?”

The fun comes from the table talk: accusations, denials and the moment everyone suddenly becomes suspicious of the quiet player.
