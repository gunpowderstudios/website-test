King of the Kastles

Small box. Big siege.

Build your castle while trying to make sure everyone else’s falls apart first.

Kastles is designed to get onto the table quickly: build with cards, attack with catapults and keep an eye on every rival around you. The rules are simple enough to pick up fast, but there is plenty of room for sneaky timing and tactical play.

Your cards can become the rocks you fire from your catapults or the cards you need in hand — so deciding when to spend them is part of the fun.

2–4players

How to play Kastles.

Build, defend and attack. The rules are quick; deciding who to hit is the difficult bit.

1

Build your Kastle.

Use your cards to put together your castle and get your defences ready.

2

Load the catapults.

Cards are also your ammunition, so choose carefully what you keep and what you fire.

3

Attack a rival.

Pick your target and start knocking their castle down before they do the same to yours.

4

Stay standing.

Outlast the other players and prove who really is King of the Kastles.

“Never trust your neighbours.”

It is fast, confrontational and deliberately easy to teach — ideal when you want a proper game without a long rules explanation.

Cards do double duty.

The cards you hold are not just your options — they can also become rocks for the catapults. Spend too freely and you may leave yourself short; hold back too long and someone else may smash through first.
