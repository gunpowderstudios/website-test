A game for pushy rabbits

Grab the carrots. Mind your neighbours.

The board looks friendly. The rabbits are not always so polite.

Rabbito is a family strategy game where positioning matters. Move around the board, collect what you need and keep an eye on the other rabbits as everyone competes for the best spaces and the tastiest carrots.

The rules are approachable enough for family play, but every move can change what the next player is able to do.

2–4players

How to play Rabbito.

Move, collect and interfere — the fun comes from choosing when to chase your own plan and when to get in somebody else's way.

1

Move your rabbit.

Choose your route across the board and look for the best places to reach next.

2

Collect carrots.

Build up what you need while watching which carrots the other players are chasing.

3

Push your luck.

Good positioning can help you — and make life much harder for the rabbits around you.

4

Outsmart the burrow.

Keep adapting until one rabbit has made the most of the garden.
